Woojin Personal Assistant

A private, single-user automation tool.

Woojin Personal Assistant is a personal desktop automation tool operated by a single individual for their own use. It connects to the owner's own Google account to help organize email, calendar events, and documents.

What it does

  • Reads and summarizes the owner's own email messages
  • Reads, creates, and edits the owner's own calendar events
  • Reads, creates, and edits the owner's own Google Docs, Sheets, and Drive files

Who uses it

Only the owner of the Google account. There are no other users, no sign-up, and no public access. The application runs locally on the owner's computer.

Data handling

Data is processed on the owner's own device and is never sold, shared, or transferred to any third party. OAuth credentials are stored locally on that device. See the Privacy Policy for details.
